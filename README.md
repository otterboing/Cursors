Just a full set of cursors(based on css) that I made today.
Feel free to use them however you want with/without credit. (:

The cursors were made in [PaintDotNet](https://www.getpaint.net/) and the animations were made in [firealpaca](https://firealpaca.com/)

 The `wait` and `progress` images are APNGs <img width="32" src="64px/progress.png" /> <img width="32" src="64px/wait.png" />

The `.mdp` files are opened in [FireAlpaca](https://firealpaca.com/)

I used [FSresizer](https://www.faststone.org/FSResizerDownload.htm) to resize the smaller versions.
